# World Style

Required:
Clear landmarks / Attractive lighting / Comfortable movement / Good camera composition / Distinct area identity / Consistent visual language

Avoid:
Generic empty Roblox city / excessive clutter / huge buildings hiding characters / unnecessary realism / performance-heavy decoration without gameplay value


## Whole-World Art Direction — undecided

Candidate directions include Korean-inspired streets/markets/signage, American-vintage details, global pop-stage design, modern resort, and dreamlike fantasy environments. Do not declare one direction the proven demand winner based on anecdote.

Next design task: create 2–3 moodboards and compare visual identity, avatar readability, route continuity, asset workload, and how clearly each theme communicates to international players. Korean details such as Hangul signs, a fish-shaped pastry stall, market alleys or a hanok cafe should serve a spatial/story purpose rather than being scattered decorations.

The Fashion Week Backstage exterior (camper/green-room trailer, tent, or permanent building) remains open until the surrounding world style is chosen.
