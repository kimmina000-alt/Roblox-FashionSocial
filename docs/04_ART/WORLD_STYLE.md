# World Style

Required:
Clear landmarks / Attractive lighting / Comfortable movement / Good camera composition / Distinct area identity / Consistent visual language

Avoid:
Generic empty Roblox city / excessive clutter / huge buildings hiding characters / unnecessary realism / performance-heavy decoration without gameplay value


## Whole-World Art Direction — decided 2026-10-20 (D-046)

User decision: the world uses a **general modern city** look (the shared feel of today's big cities: clean streets, glass and concrete, shop fronts, signage and night lighting), not a specific country. **One district is a Hanok Village** (한옥 마을): tiled roofs, stone walls, wooden pillars, a gate, a pond pavilion, lantern-lit alleys at night. American-vintage and whole-world fantasy are no longer whole-world candidates (fantasy remains inside Playground/Aquarium as those spaces' own theme).

Moodboard (references found 2026-10-20, for mood only — do not copy photos or brands into the game):
- Hanok Village: Bukchon Hanok Village alleys (Seoul) — narrow stone alleys, tiled roof lines; Jeonju Hanok Village at night — lanterns, warm light; palace pond pavilions (jeongja) for the photo landmark.
- City: Seoul/Seongsu-style modern streets — concept-store fronts, night neon, wide plazas; stylised Roblox city packs for scale and modular parts.
- Hanok details should serve a spatial purpose: gate = landmark/photo, alleys = slow walk, pavilion = photo + sitting, lanterns = night mood. Houses are not enterable small rooms (no private spaces, D-043).

The Fashion Week Backstage exterior (camper/green-room trailer, tent, or permanent building) remains open until the surrounding world style is chosen.
