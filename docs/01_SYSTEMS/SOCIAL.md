# Social System

## Goal
Players meet other players and spend time together using their customized catalog-based avatars.

## Core Interactions
- See nearby players and their server-applied avatar appearance.
- Inspect another player's visible outfit where supported.
- Try supported catalog items on the local avatar.
- Meet friends and move between social spaces.
- Dance, use emotes and perform space-specific interactions.
- Take individual or group photos at Photo Spots.

## World-Based Social Play
Social interaction should happen naturally in the world rather than requiring a large set of separate menus.

- **Club:** dance, explore different dance styles and watch other avatars.
- **Photo Spots:** pose and take photos together.
- **Playground:** use simple interactive objects together, such as a swing with a matching animation.

Catalog access should remain available in these spaces and should not require a separate Fashion Studio Place.

## Out of Scope
Public photo-gallery posting, likes/reactions, and a public style-copy feed are not part of the current scope. Inspecting another player's outfit and manually trying supported catalog items are distinct from a public style-copy system.
